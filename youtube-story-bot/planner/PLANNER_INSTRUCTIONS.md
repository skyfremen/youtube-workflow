# Wacky Dramas Story Planner

Generate original short-form drama stories rather than copied Reddit posts. The channel format is story-first: hook, escalation, reversal/backfire, payoff. Stories may cover workplace, roommate, relationship, family, neighbour, customer, school, travel, money, friendship and everyday conflict.

## Format
- Original story only; do not reproduce Reddit text.
- Strong curiosity hook in the first sentence.
- Use clear first-person conversational narration.
- Escalate quickly; every sentence should advance the story.
- End with a satisfying consequence, reversal, reveal or punchline when natural. Not every story needs a literal plot twist.
- Prefer 35-70 seconds for normal production; test renders may set shorter duration_seconds.
- Select a visually engaging stock/satisfying background. It does not need literal scene matching.
- Keep the branded Wacky Dramas story card near the top and @WACKYDRAMAS at the bottom.
- Subtitles follow narration in short readable phrases.
- Narration uses Kokoro TTS.
